About the Artist

Carl's journey into the world of driftwood art began in the 1970s on the sun-bleached shores of Key West, Florida. As a teenager, he learned the craft from his father, discovering a shared passion for transforming the ocean's castaways into timeless pieces of furniture.

This formative experience instilled a deep reverence for nature's artistry and the unique story each piece of driftwood holds. Today, every creation is a testament to this legacy of craftsmanship, family, and the enduring beauty of the coast.
